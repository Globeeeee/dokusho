## What's new

### Listening (read aloud)

- **Long press any paragraph to start listening from there.** If the voice is already reading
  that chapter, it jumps to the paragraph.
- **Supertonic sounds smoother:** sentences follow each other with no gap, "..." reads as a
  pause, and very short lines join the next sentence.
- **Supertonic reads 31 languages** (English, Japanese, Korean, Spanish, French, German,
  Russian, Vietnamese and more), using the novel's language. For a language it can't read
  (Chinese, Thai), the system voice takes over and a message tells you.
- **The highlight follows the voice:** the spoken sentence stays in the middle of the screen
  in scroll mode, page modes turn to its page, and you can pick the highlight colour.
- **Save spoken audio:** sentences you have heard are kept on the phone (up to 300 MB), so a
  chapter played again starts at once. Supertonic always does this; for system voices it is a
  setting.
- **Play sample** in the read aloud settings lets you hear a voice, speed and pitch before
  reading.
- **Engine status:** a line under the engine says whether it is ready, has no voice for your
  language (with an **Install voices** button), or needs internet.
- Chapters finished by the voice stay marked as read, and a notification sound lowers the voice
  instead of restarting the sentence.

### Reading

- **Your own text files:** put TXT, HTML or EPUB novels in Local source. A text EPUB shows one
  chapter per section of the book, with its pictures. Listen and export work on them too.
- **Tap zones** from the reader settings (L shaped, Kindle-ish, Edge, Right and left, off, and
  inverted) now work in the novel reader, and are in the novel Reading mode tab.
- Book-style **page turn effect** for the left to right and right to left page modes.

### Settings and wording

- Settings say "novel" instead of "entry" everywhere in English, and the library export file is
  named `dokusho_library.csv`.
- Download settings that only apply to manga images are hidden.
- The backup screen no longer shows an empty Tracking option.

## Fixes

- **Fixed a crash** when changing the voice or pitch while Supertonic was reading.
- **Earbud play and pause** now work with system voices (Google, Samsung), not only Supertonic.
- **A voice engine that fails no longer stops reading:** Dokusho switches to the phone's default
  engine and tells you.
- Engines that need a paid subscription (eReader Prestigio) are no longer listed.
- Changing pitch or speed with the slider no longer restarts the sentence on every step.

## Installing

Update over your current version; your library and progress stay. If Google Play Protect says
**Harmful app blocked**, that is a false alarm on new, unknown apps that install extensions, as
Mihon also sees. Tap **More details** and **Install anyway** if offered, or turn off
**Scan apps with Play Protect** for the install and turn it back on afterwards.
