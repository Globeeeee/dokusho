# Getting started with Dokusho

Dokusho reads web novels from sites through **extensions**, the same way Mihon reads comics.
The app has no novels of its own until you install at least one extension.

## 1. Add the extension store (once)

1. Open **More** → **Settings** → **Browse** → **Extension stores**.
2. Tap **Add extension store** and paste this into **Store index URL**:

   ```
   https://github.com/Globeeeee/dokusho-extensions/raw/repo/index.pb
   ```

3. Confirm. The store stays saved; you never need to add it again.

## 2. Install extensions

Go to **Browse** → **Extensions** and tap **Install** on the sites you read:

| Extension | Good for |
|-----------|----------|
| Royal Road | Original English web serials (The Wandering Inn, Mother of Learning) |
| Scribble Hub | Original English and translated web novels |
| Wattpad | Free stories; paid Wattpad Originals parts stay locked |
| WuxiaWorld | Official translations; locked chapters are marked 🔒 and need your own account |
| Project Gutenberg | Free public-domain classics |
| Syosetu | Japanese web novels (小説家になろう) |

Updates for extensions show up on the same screen.

## 3. Find a novel and add it to your library

1. **Browse** → **Sources**, tap a site. Use **Popular**, **Latest** or the search icon.
2. **Global search** (the search icon on **Browse**) checks every installed site at once, handy
   to see which site has the novel you want.
3. Open a novel and tap **Add to library** (the heart). It now shows on the **Library** tab.

## 4. Read

- Tap a chapter to open the reader. Tap the middle of the screen for the menu.
- **Reading mode**: continuous scroll (chapters flow into each other) or paged left to right,
  right to left and vertical.
- **Settings** (gear in the reader menu): font, size, line height, margins, paragraph spacing,
  justify, indent, and themes (paper, sepia, dark, black, or your own colors).
- Progress is saved per paragraph, so a chapter reopens where you stopped.

## 5. Read offline

Open a novel, long press chapters (or use the download icon) and pick **Download**. Downloaded
chapters, including their images, open without internet. **More** → **Download queue** shows
progress.

## 6. Stay up to date

The **Updates** tab lists new chapters for novels in your library. Pull down on **Library** to
check now; **Settings** → **Library** sets automatic checks.

## Moving a novel to another site

If a site stops working, open **Browse** → **Migrate**, pick the old site and the novel, then
search another installed site. Reading progress and categories move with it.

## Tracking

Trackers (AniList, MyAnimeList and others) are switched off in Dokusho for now. Your library
and progress stay on the phone. Back them up from **More** → **Settings** → **Data and storage** → **Create backup**.

## Something not working?

See [troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md): Cloudflare checks, sites blocked on your network,
update errors and storage.
