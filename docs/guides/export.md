# Export

Save a novel as an EPUB or PDF file to read in another app, on an e-reader or on a computer.

## Export one novel

1. Open the novel's page.
2. Tap the three-dot menu at the top right, then **Export**.
3. Choose the options and tap **Export**.

**Export** only shows for novels from text sources (novel extensions).

## Export several novels

**More** → **Settings** → **Data and storage** → **Novel export** → **Export novels** lists the
novels in your library. Search with the magnifier, tap novels to select them, or tap the select
all icon to select every novel the list shows (tap it again to clear). Tap **Export**, choose the
options once, and they apply to every selected novel. The novels export one after another.

## Options

| Option | What it does |
|--------|--------------|
| **Format** | **EPUB** (default) or **PDF**. PDF pages are A5 with page numbers. |
| **Chapters** | **All chapters**, **Downloaded chapters**, or **From chapter to chapter**. Chapter numbers count from 1, the first chapter in reading order. |
| **Chapters per file** | How many chapters go in one file. 0 puts the whole book in one file. |
| **Download missing chapters** | Fetches chapters that are not downloaded or cached, at the source's usual pace. Off: those chapters are skipped. |

Text comes from downloaded chapters first, then chapters you opened recently. Images are
included when they are downloaded or already loaded once; with **Download missing chapters** on,
missing images are fetched too. Locked chapters are never exported.

## Defaults

**More** → **Settings** → **Data and storage** → **Novel export** sets what the options start
with: **Default format**, **EPUB chapters per file** (default: whole book in one file),
**PDF chapters per file** (default: 100) and **Download missing chapters**.

## Progress and files

A notification shows which novel is exporting and how many chapters are done; tap **Cancel** in
it to stop. When it finishes, **Open** opens the file and **Share** sends the files to another
app. Skipped chapters are listed with the reason: locked, not downloaded, or couldn't load.

Files are saved in your storage location, in `exports/<Title>/`, named like
`<Title> - ch0001-0100.epub`. Exporting the same chapters again replaces the old file. The storage
location is set under **More** → **Settings** → **Data and storage** → **Storage location**.
