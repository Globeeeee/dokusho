# Reader

Tap a chapter to open it. Tap the middle of the screen to show or hide the menu.

## Reading modes

| Mode | How it reads |
|------|--------------|
| **Scroll** | One long strip. The next chapter follows straight after the current one |
| **Pages, left to right** | Swipe or tap right to go forward |
| **Pages, right to left** | Swipe or tap left to go forward |
| **Pages, top to bottom** | Swipe up to go forward |

Switch with the reading mode button in the bottom bar (each tap moves to the next mode), in the
gear menu under **Reading mode**, or as a default in **More** → **Settings** → **Reader** →
**Novel reader**.

## Text settings

Open the gear in the reader menu. The **Reading mode** tab holds the text options:

| Setting | Range |
|---------|-------|
| **Font** | Serif, Sans-serif, Monospace, System default |
| **Font size** | 12 to 32 |
| **Line spacing** | 1.2 to 2.2 |
| **Paragraph spacing** | 0 to 40 |
| **Margins** | 8 to 48 |
| **Justify text** | On or off |
| **Indent first line of paragraphs** | On or off |
| **Theme** | Same as background, Paper, Sepia, Dark, Black |

**Same as background** follows **General** → **Background color**: White gives Paper, Gray gives
Dark, Black gives Black, and Auto switches between Paper and Black with your phone's dark mode.

For your own colors, open the **Custom filter** tab and turn on **Custom text and background
colors**. Pick a preset (Paper, Sepia, Night, Black) or set red, green and blue for the background
and the text. The same tab has **Custom brightness** to dim the screen below the system minimum.

## Tap zones and volume keys

- In page modes, the left third goes back and the right third goes forward (reversed for right
  to left). In **Pages, top to bottom**, the top third goes back and the bottom third forward.
- In **Scroll**, the left and right thirds scroll up or down by most of a screen.
- The middle third always opens the menu.
- Turn on **Volume keys** in **More** → **Settings** → **Reader** → **Navigation** to turn pages
  or scroll with the volume buttons. **Invert volume keys** swaps them.

The image reader's tap zone layouts do not apply to novels.

## General options

The **General** tab of the gear menu:

- **Show page number**: the percentage of the chapter in **Scroll**, or page and total in page
  modes.
- **Keep screen on** while reading.
- **Fullscreen** and **Show content in cutout area**.

## Chapters and progress

- Progress is saved per paragraph, so a chapter reopens where you stopped.
- In **Scroll**, reading past the end runs into the next chapter, and it is marked read.
- In page modes, the last page shows **End of chapter** with a **Next chapter** button; turning
  past it opens the next chapter. **You are up to date** means there is no next chapter yet.
- The next chapter starts loading once you are past 60 percent of the current one.

## Images in chapters

Pictures in a chapter show where the site places them, including animated GIF and WebP. Online
they load from the site; downloaded chapters keep their images, see [downloads](downloads.md).

## Listen (read aloud)

Open the menu and tap **Listen**. Reading starts at the paragraph at the top of the screen. To
start somewhere else, long press that paragraph: reading starts there, or jumps there if the
voice is already reading this chapter.

- The sentence being read is highlighted, with a stronger mark on the current word. Pick the
  colour in the reader menu → **General** → **Read-aloud highlight** (Auto uses your text colour).
- The reader follows the voice, in the way that suits the reading mode:

  | Reading mode | What it does |
  |--------------|--------------|
  | Scroll | Keeps the sentence being read in the middle of the screen |
  | Pages (any direction) | Turns to the page that holds the sentence being read |

- Scroll or turn pages yourself and it stops following; any playback button, or the sentence
  being read coming back on screen, picks it up again.
- At the end of a chapter the voice marks it read and carries on into the next one.

### Controls

A bar floats over the text while listening:

| Button | Does |
|--------|------|
| Previous / next | Jump one paragraph |
| Play / pause | Pause and resume |
| Clock | **Sleep timer**: off, 5, 15, 30, 45 or 60 minutes |
| Speed (1×) | Steps through 0.75× to 2.5× |
| Gear | Read aloud settings |
| Close | Stop |

### In the background

Listening keeps going with the screen off or in another app. The notification has previous
paragraph, play or pause, next paragraph, **Next chapter** and stop. Headset buttons work.
Playback pauses for calls and other audio, and when headphones are unplugged.

### Read aloud settings

From the gear on the listen bar, the gear menu's **Reading mode** tab, or **More** →
**Settings** → **Reader** → **Read aloud settings**:

- **Engine**: **System default**, each text to speech app installed on the phone (for example
  Google Speech Services), or **Supertonic**. Engines that need a paid subscription (eReader
  Prestigio) are not listed. A line under the engine says whether it is ready, has no voice for
  your language (tap **Install voices**), or needs internet. If the chosen engine fails, reading
  carries on with the system default and a message says so.
- **Voice**: for system engines, tap the card to open the voice list, grouped by language.
  **Online** voices need internet; **HD** marks higher quality voices.
- **Speed**: 0.5× to 3×. **Pitch**: 0.5× to 2×.
- **Save spoken audio** (system engines): each sentence is kept on the phone, up to 300 MB, so a
  chapter played again starts at once without the engine. Supertonic always does this.
- **Play sample**: hear the engine, voice, speed and pitch before reading. Hidden while a
  chapter is being read aloud, since changes are heard there.

### Supertonic

Supertonic is a natural sounding voice that runs on the phone, with no internet once it is
downloaded.

1. Tap **Supertonic** under **Engine**. It downloads in the background (about 400 MB) and the
   chip shows progress.
2. Until it finishes, speech keeps using a system voice.
3. When **Supertonic is ready to read aloud** appears, tap **Supertonic** again to use it.
4. Pick one of ten voices: Female 1 to 5 or Male 1 to 5.

Supertonic reads English, Japanese and Korean text. For other languages, use a system engine
with a voice for that language. If **No text-to-speech engine is available on this device**
shows, install one (such as Google Speech Services) from the Play Store.
